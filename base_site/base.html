You link your recurring bills (rent, car payment, utilities, etc.) and choose how often you want to contribute: daily, weekly, or biweekly. We automatically set aside small amounts each period, and when your bill comes due, we pay it on time using the accumulated funds.

Fractional charges a flat 1.5% transaction fee on each contribution and bill payment. There are no monthly subscriptions, no plan tiers, and no hidden fees. For example, paying a $200 bill costs just $3.00 in fees. You only pay when you use the service.

Absolutely. Your funds are held in FDIC-insured accounts through our banking partner. We use bank-level 256-bit encryption, and our platform is SOC 2 Type II compliant. We connect to your bank securely through Plaid, the same technology used by Venmo, Robinhood, and Coinbase.

We'll never overdraft your account. If your balance is low, we skip the contribution and notify you. You can make it up later or adjust your schedule. Our overdraft protection feature monitors your balance and pauses contributions automatically when needed.
